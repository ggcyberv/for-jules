Overview
8 equipment slots: Weapon, Armor, Helm, Gloves, Boots, Amulet, Ring (x2), Shield (optional)

Gear influences derived stats (HP, mana, attack, resistances, dodge, speed) and can also modify base stats (rarely)

No gear overrides core identity – maximum total bonus from gear ≈ +30–40% to a derived stat at high levels, not 300%

Weapons add flat damage to Physical/Magical Attack (additive, not multiplicative)

Gear rarity determines number of affixes

1. Gear Slots & Primary Stat Focus
Slot	Primary Affects	Secondary Affects
Weapon	Physical/Magical Attack (base damage), Crit Chance	Special effects (life steal, elemental damage)
Armor (Chest)	Armor, HP	Magical Resist, Size-based penalties
Helm	Perception, Magical Resist	Mana, Crit Chance
Gloves	Agility, Crit Chance/Damage	Physical Attack (small)
Boots	Speed, Dodge	Constitution (small)
Amulet	Charisma, All Resistances	Mana regen, XP gain
Ring	Variable (STR/INT/CON/AGI)	HP, Mana, Resistances
Shield	Armor, Block Chance	Strength, Physical Resist
2. Weapon Types (Detailed)
Weapons provide base damage range added to Physical Attack or Magical Attack.

Type	Damage Range	Scaling with Base Stats	Special Property
Dagger	6–12	50% STR + 50% AGI	+5% Crit Chance, +10% Crit Damage
Sword	10–18	70% STR + 30% AGI	+5% Accuracy (reduces dodge)
Axe	14–22	100% STR	+10% Armor Penetration
Mace	12–20	90% STR	10% chance to stun for 0.5s
Spear	9–15	60% STR + 40% AGI	+5% Dodge, +1 Reach (hits back row)
Staff	8–14	100% INT	+10% Magical Attack, +5% Mana
Wand	5–9	80% INT + 20% PER	+10% Mana Regen, +5% Crit Chance (spells)
Bow	8–14	100% AGI	Ranged, +10% Crit Damage
Crossbow	12–18	100% AGI	Ranged, -10% Speed, +15% Armor Penetration
Two-handed weapons (Greatsword, Greataxe, Longbow) get +50% base damage but occupy both hands (no shield).

Damage calculation example:

text
Physical Attack = (STR × 3) + (Level × 1.5) + Weapon_Base_Damage
Total damage output = Physical Attack × (1 + Crit_Damage_if_crit)
3. Armor Types & Base Values
Armor (chest slot) provides base Armor value, then affixes add more.

Armor Type	Base Armor	Speed Penalty	Dodge Penalty	Bonus Effect
Cloth	8–15	0	0%	+10% Mana Regen
Leather	16–30	-2	-5%	+5% Dodge
Chain	31–50	-8	-15%	+5% Physical Resist
Plate	51–80	-15	-30%	+10% Armor, -10% Magical Resist
Shield (optional): Adds +15–40 Armor and 15–25% Block Chance (block = 50% damage reduction). Shields also have -5% to -15% dodge penalty.

5. Gear Progression & Scaling
Gear drops scale with area level / enemy level. Item level = zone level (1–100+).

Base item value formula:

text
Base_Armor = Type_Base × (1 + (Item_Level - 1) × 0.02)   [capped at +200% at level 100]
Weapon_Damage = Type_Min_Max × (1 + (Item_Level - 1) × 0.015) [capped at +150%]
Example: A level 20 Plate Armor base armor = 65 × (1 + 19×0.02) = 65 × 1.38 ≈ 90 armor.

Affix magnitude scales with item level:
Affix_Value = Base_Affix_Value × (1 + (Item_Level - 1) × 0.01) up to +100% at level 100.

6. Interaction with Character Stats (Balance Rules)
To ensure gear doesn’t trivialize base stats:

Gear cannot increase a base stat by more than +50% of the character’s natural value at that level.
(E.g., level 20 character with 17 CON → gear max +8 CON total from all slots.)

Damage stacking:
Weapon damage is additive with Physical Attack, not multiplicative.
Total_Physical_Damage = (STR×3 + Level×1.5) + Weapon_Base_Damage + Sum_Flat_Bonuses
→ This means a great weapon at level 20 adds ~30–40% extra damage, not 300%.

Dodge cap still enforced: Gear can push you to the cap (50% base, modified by size).
Example: Halfling (65% cap) can reach 65% with gear.

Armor diminishing returns:
Damage reduction = Armor / (Armor + 200).
Gear can push Armor from 50 (20% reduction) to 200 (50% reduction) – meaningful but not god mode.

Rarity vs Level trade-off: A level 10 Epic (2 affixes at 2.0x strength) might be better than a level 20 Common (0 affixes). Encourages farming.

7. Automated Combat & Gear Synergy
Since combat is automated, gear should enable archetypes without player micro:

Archetype	Gear Focus
Tank	Plate Armor + Shield, affixes: HP, Armor, Block, Constitution
Berserker	Chain Armor + Two-handed Axe, affixes: Strength, Crit Damage, Life Leech
Rogue	Leather Armor + Daggers, affixes: Agility, Dodge, Crit Chance
Mage	Cloth Armor + Staff, affixes: Intelligence, Mana, Magical Resist, Mana Regen
Ranger	Leather Armor + Bow, affixes: Perception, Agility, Crit Chance
Paladin	Plate Armor + Sword/Shield, affixes: Charisma, HP, All Resistances
AI behavior can be slightly influenced by gear (e.g., high Block → AI prefers taunt; high Crit → focus low-HP enemies).

Resulting stats vs naked level 20 (parentheses):

HP: 480 (418) → +62

Armor: 68 + 40+85+15+45 = 253 → Damage reduction 253/453 = 56% (vs 25% naked)

Dodge: 10% base -30% plate -10% shield +5% boots = -25% (negative → 0% dodge, as intended for tank)

Speed: -15% plate +12% boots = -3% net (slow but fine)

Physical Attack: 81 + 15 = 96

Very tanky, low dodge, moderate damage – balanced.

9. Crafting & Upgrade Systems (Optional)
To give player agency without breaking automation:

Enchanting Table: Reroll one affix on a rare+ item (cost: gold + rare material)

Upgrade Orbs: Increase item level by +5 (up to zone level cap), scaling base values and affixes

Set Items (3–5 pieces): Provide unique bonuses (e.g., “+20% damage to bleeding enemies”)

Gems/Sockets: Insert into weapon/armor for small stat boosts (e.g., +2% dodge, +5% crit damage)
